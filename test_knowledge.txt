VoxAgent is an AI voice assistant platform designed for B2B agencies. 
It supports inbound and outbound calls, fallback messaging via SMS and WhatsApp using Twilio, and advanced voice AI using VAPI.
Agencies can create multiple AI assistants, configure their system prompts, voices (e.g., Nova, Alloy), and link them to knowledge bases for dynamic responses.
Billing is handled via Stripe, with plans including Starter ($49/mo), Growth ($149/mo), and Pro ($399/mo).
Overage minutes are billed at $0.15/min at the end of the billing cycle.
